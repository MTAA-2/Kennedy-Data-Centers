Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of workers does this cover?

Any hourly trade on a data center project: electricians, laborers, cable and fiber installers, pipefitters, HVAC techs, carpenters, ironworkers, operators, and more. Union or nonunion. Hired direct or through a staffing agency.

What extra pay might count toward my overtime rate?

Under federal law, many bonuses, and some per diem and other extra pay, might need to be counted in your overtime rate — including attendance, safety, completion, retention, or sign-on bonuses, per diem paid by the hour, and night or weekend shift differentials.

What if I was paid by the day, or worked as a contractor?

The check covers different pay arrangements, including hourly work, day rate, salary, flat weekly pay, and contractor / 1099 work.
