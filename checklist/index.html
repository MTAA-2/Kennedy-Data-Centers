Why the Overtime Rate Might Be Wrong

Under federal law, many bonuses, and some per diem and other extra pay, might need to be counted in your overtime rate, not just your base hourly wage. When that pay is left out, every overtime hour might be paid short.

For electricians, laborers, cable and fiber installers, pipefitters, HVAC techs, carpenters, ironworkers, operators, commissioning techs, and other hourly workers. Union or nonunion. Hired direct or through a staffing agency.
